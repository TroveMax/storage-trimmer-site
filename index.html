Privacy

Your photos never leave your device.

Storage Trimmer was built with a simple rule: your photos & videos are yours. The app makes zero network requests. There are no accounts, no servers, and nothing to sign up for.

  • No network requests The app makes zero outbound connections. It works completely offline.
  • No analytics or tracking No crash reporters, no usage analytics, no telemetry of any kind.
  • On-device ML only Apple Vision runs locally. Image data is never extracted or transmitted.
  • No third-party SDKs Only Apple’s own frameworks. No third-party code with hidden data practices.
How it works

Three steps to a cleaner library

Storage Trimmer does the hard work so you don’t have to scroll through thousands of near-identical shots.

01

Scan your library or folder

Choose a date range or scan your entire library. Storage Trimmer analyses every photo and video using Apple’s on-device ML — no uploads, ever.

02

Review the clusters

Similar photos & videos are grouped together. You can sort clusters by time or by size of clusters. When you view an asset you can zoom in and see the metadata.

All grouping and scoring runs locally — no image data is ever sent anywhere.

03

Organize, Share, and or delete to free up space

We give you features to organize the photos by moving them into new albums, to share memories with friends, and to trim things up and delete what you don't want to keep. For Apple Photos, it keeps deleted items in Recently Deleted for 30 days, just in case.

Deletions go through Apple Photos. We never have direct access to your files.

Features

Smarter than a simple file compare

Storage Trimmer understands your photos & videos, not just their file sizes.

Visual similarity detection

Uses Apple Vision’s neural feature prints to find photos & videos that look alike — even if they’re different exposures, crops, or edits of the same moment.

Organise your memories

While walking down memory lane, share photos & videos with friends, save the best to Favourites or Albums, or delete the ones where your eyes are closed.

Video support

Analyses video clips too, sampling multiple frames to find near-duplicate recordings that are eating your storage.

Flexible date ranges

Scan just the last 30 days, a custom date range, or your entire library. Large libraries are processed in chunks to stay fast and responsive.

Fast, non-blocking

Processing runs entirely in the background. Your Mac stays responsive while Storage Trimmer works through even the largest libraries.

Track your trimming

As you make your way through your clusters and start deleting the excess, you can keep track of how much data you've deleted from the Home tab.
